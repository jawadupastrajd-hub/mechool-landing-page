(06) FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Everything you need to know about our AC deep cleaning process and service warranty.

How often should I have my air conditioner deep cleaned?

We recommend a full chemical deep jet wash every 6 months for residential units in warm climates. For high-usage commercial environments or homes with pets/allergies, a 4-month maintenance cycle keeps air pure and power consumption low.

Is the chemical cleaning solution safe for children and pets?

Yes, absolutely. We use eco-friendly, biodegradable, non-corrosive cleaning agents that are specially formulated to dissolve grime without releasing toxic chemical vapors. Your rooms are safe for immediate use after cleaning.

Will water or chemical spray splash on my walls or furniture?

Not at all. Our technicians install specialized heavy-duty waterproof funnel bags directly beneath your AC unit before spraying. All wastewater drains cleanly into closed collection buckets, protecting your walls, curtains, and flooring 100%.

How long does a split AC deep jet wash take?

A thorough single split AC chemical jet wash takes approximately 45 to 60 minutes per unit. This includes disassembly, coil wash, drainage flush, anti-bacterial fogging, reassembly, and operational testing.

What is covered under the 30-Day Service Guarantee?

Our 30-day guarantee covers any water leakage, drainage blockages, or unexpected cooling performance issues after service. If any problem arises within 30 days, we send a technician to re-inspect and fix it free of charge.
